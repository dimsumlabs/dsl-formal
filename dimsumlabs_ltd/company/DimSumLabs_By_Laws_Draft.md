# Dim Sum Labs Maker Club Limited - Operational By-laws

## Introduction and Governance Model

Dim Sum Labs ("DSL") operates under a **do-ocracy** model with aspects of democracy. We believe that those who do the work get to make the decisions about that work. 
While consensus is valued, we intentionally avoid extreme consensus models that cause gridlock. Instead, we empower active participants to take initiative, provided their actions align with our Objects, the Articles of Association, and these By-laws.

## Code of Conduct

All individuals interacting with DSL—whether online, in our physical space, or at events—must adhere to our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/dimsumlabs/dsl-formal/blob/master/code_of_conduct.md).
Adherence to the Code of Conduct is a strict requirement for all Membership tiers and guests.

## Political Neutrality

Dim Sum Labs is a space dedicated to making, creating, and learning.
To preserve this environment:
*   We remain strictly and perpetually politically neutral.
*   No Member, Director, or Club Member may represent DSL in commenting on or participating in political movements or events (e.g., local protests or riots).
*   **Exception:** DSL may collectively advocate or chime in *only* on specific policy issues that directly impact our core operations as a makerspace (e.g., technology, STEM education, open-source software, or AI legislation).

## Club Membership

DSL establishes the following non-statutory operational membership tiers ("Club Members"):

### Paying Club Members

To accommodate different needs and capacities, the Paying Club Member tier is subdivided as follows:

*   **Full Time:** HKD 700 per month. Grants 24/7 key access to the physical DSL space.
*   **Student / Hardship Concession:** HKD 300 per month. Grants 24/7 key access, subject to a brief review of eligibility.

**Common Privileges for all Paying sub-tiers:**
*   **Qualifications:** Any person who agrees to the Code of Conduct and pays the monthly dues for their respective tier is eligible.
*   **Equipment:** Unrestricted access to maker equipment (e.g., 3D printers, laser cutters, electronics stations) during their permitted access hours, provided they have completed required safety and operational training.
*   **Guest Privileges:** May bring a maximum of two (2) guests to the space at any time during their permitted access hours (limit does not apply during officially sanctioned public events). The hosting Member is strictly responsible for the conduct and safety of their guests.
*   **Event Hosting and Workshops:** May host their own events at the space and participate in DSL-sponsored workshops at discounted rates.

#### Member Onboarding Process

Becoming a Paying Club Member involves the following steps:
1.  **Community Engagement:** Prospective members are expected to attend a few public events (like HackJams) or engage in the IRC/Telegram groups to understand the culture and get to know existing members.
2.  **Sponsorship/Vetting:** The prospective member must be introduced by an existing Paying Club Member or Director. 
3.  **Financial Commitment:** The prospective member agrees to pay the monthly dues.
4.  **Induction:** The new member will receive an orientation regarding the Code of Conduct, safety rules, space etiquette, and key/access provisioning.

## Space Access and Project Storage Policy

### Access Control
DSL maintains an electronic door access system for the premises. Paying Club Members are registered on the system and issued access credentials based on their membership tier. ## Operational Voting and "Do-ocracy" Resolutions

Because DSL is a do-ocracy, active members are empowered to make day-to-day decisions. If a participant wants to improve the space (e.g., buying a new tool, rearranging furniture, or creating a new storage system), the following consensus-seeking procedure applies:

1.  **Proposal:** Propose the idea publicly on the main communication channel (IRC/Telegram) or via the mailing list, explaining the cost, effort, and benefits.
2.  **Lazy Consensus (The 72-Hour Rule):** If the change is minor (e.g., spending small amounts of club funds, minor layout changes), the proposal is considered accepted if no Paying Club Member raises a substantial objection within 72 hours.
3.  **Active Voting:** For major changes (e.g., significant expenditures, permanent alterations to the space structure, or binding organizational affiliations):
    *   A formal attempt to find consensus must be made. 
    *   If consensus cannot be reached after two (2) weeks of trying, a fallback vote is triggered so the space is not paralyzed by extreme gridlock.
    *   Voting may take place via the mailing list, instant messaging poll, or at a HackJam.
    *   A majority of 2/3 (two-thirds) of participating voters approves the measure, provided at least 15% of total Paying Club Members participate in the vote to form a quorum.
4.  **Dispute Resolution:** In the rare event of an unresolvable operational dispute among Club Members that threatens the organization legally or financially, the statutory Directors reserve the right to make a final, binding decision to protect the integrity and liability of Dim Sum Labs Maker Club Limited.
